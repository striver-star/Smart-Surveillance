# Management guide

For the General Manager and other management accounts. You see the overview, devices and reports; you don't handle alerts.

## What you receive

- **WhatsApp**: the reports you subscribed to (daily, weekly, monthly) and, if set, critical alerts and escalations when an alert isn't handled in time.
- **Reports** (in the console): every report, searchable, as PDF or Word.

## Reading a report

- **Summary**: events by severity and the change from the previous period.
- **Critical events**: each with snapshot, place and time, and how it was handled.
- **Busiest times and places**: where to put guards.
- **Repeat alerts**: the same place alerting again and again usually means a camera or rule needs fixing.
- **Open items**: drive faults, offline cameras, clocks, failed message deliveries. Each one has an owner (normally IT).

## Incident heat map

In the console, **Incident heat map** with *Real incidents* shows where and at what hours real trouble happens: the basis for patrol and lighting decisions.

## Compliance report

Each month you receive the **compliance and privacy report**. It should show no *Action* items; ask the CSO about any that appear, and about out-of-hours video viewing by anyone who doesn't work nights.

## Questions management should ask each month

1. Were all critical alerts acknowledged within the target time?
2. Which open items are older than a week, and why?
3. Are backups and the restore test passing (IT Manager, Security page)?
4. If face and plate recognition are on: is the watchlist reviewed and the DPIA due date known?
