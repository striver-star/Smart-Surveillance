# Viewer and client guide

For read-only accounts. You can watch the cameras your account was given, and nothing else. What you
watch is recorded in the viewing log with your name.

## Live view

- Pick cameras on the left: 1, 4, 9 or 16 at a time. Double-click a tile for full screen.
- Only your cameras are listed. If one you need is missing, ask the CSO.

## Playback (if your account allows it)

- Pick a camera and a day, then click the timeline to play the recording from that time.
- Green shows when the NVR recorded; marks show events.
- If Playback isn't in your menu, your account is for live video only.

## Viewers only: alerts, events and the site map

- **Alerts** and **Events** show what happened on your cameras. You can read them and open their pictures,
  but acknowledging and closing alerts is for the control room.
- **Site map** shows your cameras and the guards on patrol.

## Viewers only: smart search

**Smart search** finds people or vehicles by colour in the pictures of past events on your cameras. Searches are recorded.

## Good practice

- Watch for the purpose your account was given; don't film the screen or share what you see.
- Sign out when you leave the computer. Your session also ends after a period without use.
