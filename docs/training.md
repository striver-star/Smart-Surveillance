# Training plan

Hands-on, at the site, on the live system. Each session ends with the competency check for that role;
sign it off in the table at the end.

| Session | Who | Length | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. The console | Everyone | 45 min | Sign in, two-factor (admin, CSO), Overview, Live view, Help page, signing out |
| 2. Handling alerts | Operators, CSO | 2 h | Alert lifecycle, acknowledge, notes, live check, closing reasons, escalation; 10 practice alerts with the walk-through and walk test |
| 3. Site map and guards | Operators, CSO, guard supervisors | 1 h | Map, zones, guard phones (Traccar Client set-up, SOS), missed patrols |
| 4. Video wall | Operators, CSO | 30 min | Screens, rotation, alert outlines; what to do if a TV is blank |
| 5. Rules, evidence and reports | CSO | 2 h | Alert rules and schedules, evidence sealing and legal hold, reports, watchlist rules (if face and plate are on) |
| 6. Administration | IT Manager and deputy | 3 h | Install and upgrade, users, NVRs and event setup, backups and restore, Security page, licence, troubleshooting |
| 7. Reports for management | GM and management | 30 min | Reading the daily, weekly and monthly reports; the monthly questions |

## Competency checks

**Operator:** acknowledges, notes and closes 5 alerts correctly; finds a camera in live view and on the
map in under 30 seconds; explains when to call the CSO; signs out.

**CSO:** changes an alert rule and its schedule; seals evidence and puts it on hold; makes a custom report;
withdraws face and plate recognition (only explains, if not unlocked).

**Administrator:** adds and edits an NVR; restores a backup to a test database; reads the Security page and
fixes one item; upgrades the system; finds a service's logs.
