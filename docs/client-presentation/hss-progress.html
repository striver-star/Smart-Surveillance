Phase 7

Face and number plate

Legal review, DPIA and signs completed; face and plate recognition unlocked. Live recognition is built: watchlists with a reason, authority and expiry for every entry, matches only on approved cameras, and an operator confirms each match before anyone acts. The volunteer walk test (stage 3) sets the accuracy threshold; Gate Arrival 02 showed faces too small, so the closer entrance cameras are used.

Phase 10

Release 2.0

Three batches built. Operations: recorded playback, sirens and lights on alerts, shift logbook, phone app with check-ins and SOS, a lockdown button, a control-room status board. Insight: false alarm dashboard, incident heat map, unusual activity, smart search by colour, monthly compliance report. Control: who watched which camera, per-camera permissions with viewer and client accounts, walls that keep running through restarts, network discovery, an open API with webhooks. Access control: Hikvision and ZKTeco doors on the console, who came through, forced-door and duress alerts on the door's camera, opening doors with a reason, doors locked in a lockdown, and the intercom pop-up; tested on simulators until the first devices are installed.
